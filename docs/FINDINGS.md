# Findings and evidence status

| Claim | Status | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| The tested suite demonstrates a general addition-specific mechanism in GPT-2 Small. | Not established | Behavioral cohorts; corrected attribution and intervention records; [`docs/RESEARCH_FLOW.md`](RESEARCH_FLOW.md) | The results do not show that no arithmetic-related representation exists. |
| Equal-operand prompts have a positive mean target-level advantage in the tested digit-addition scan. | Present in saved scores; sensitivity-dependent | [`equal_operand_primary_target_summary.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_primary_target_summary.csv); [`equal_operand_primary_inference.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_primary_inference.csv); audit A32 | Seven target levels; exact sign-flip p = 0.03125; target-bootstrap 95% interval [+0.0738, +0.4374]. Sign test p = 0.125; excluding controls with operands T±1 leaves six targets, mean +0.2561 and sign-flip p = 0.125. Not mechanistic evidence or broad generalization. |
| The equal-operand contrast has a positive mean across 20 new arithmetic targets. | Positive mean, but breadth criterion not met; implementation differs from the written plan | [`preregistered_20_target_results.json`](../data/current/preregistered_20_target_results.json); [`preregistered_20_target_summary.csv`](../data/current/preregistered_20_target_summary.csv); [`analyze_preregistered_followup.py`](../src/analyze_preregistered_followup.py); [audit A33](Audit_Trail.md#a33-twenty-target-arithmetic-analysis-added-to-the-research-sequence) | Mean +0.39392; exact one-sided sign-flip p = 0.013618; stratified 95% bootstrap interval [+0.09556, +0.71692]; 8/20 positive (40%). The mean, interval, and p-value criteria pass; the 70% breadth criterion fails. The selection bins, arithmetic-template count, and unequal-split count differ from the written plan. Six multiples of ten average +1.4313, compared with −0.0507 for the other 14 targets; this subgroup analysis is exploratory. |
| A separate 11-target set shows a positive equal-minus-unequal contrast. | Not supported by the exact one-sided tests | [`strict_unused_targets_results.json`](../data/current/strict_unused_targets_results.json); [`eleven_target_target_summary.csv`](../data/current/eleven_target_target_summary.csv); [`analyze_unused_targets.py`](../src/analyze_unused_targets.py); [audit A35](Audit_Trail.md#a35-separate-11-target-score-set) | Across six formats, mean +0.28234, 7/11 positive, exact one-sided sign-flip p = 0.175293. Arithmetic only: mean +0.26796, 6/11 positive, p = 0.186523. Both p-values exceed 0.05; the six-format average is not arithmetic-specific. |
| The equal-minus-unequal score differs across target/equal-operand decimal classes in the 552-target decomposition. | Exploratory class contrast; not an isolated roundness effect | [`roundness_exploratory/`](../data/current/roundness_exploratory/); [audit A34](Audit_Trail.md#a34-exploratory-552-target-roundness-decomposition) | Mean $A_T$: +0.0347 nats per answer token for non-round targets ($n=276$), +0.3277 for targets whose equal operand ends in 5 ($n=138$), and +0.1806 for those whose equal operand ends in 0 ($n=138$). End-5 versus non-round contrast +0.2930 nats per answer token; cluster-by-magnitude-band 95% CI [+0.1724, +0.4137], two-sided p = 1.47×10⁻⁵. Target class is aliased with operand ending, and unequal-control compositions differ. The decomposition remains exploratory; its inputs and derived outputs are archived together. |
| The effect is invariant to operand format. | Not established | [`equal_operand_format_advantages.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_format_advantages.csv); [`equal_operand_format_pairwise_tests.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_format_pairwise_tests.csv) | Digit+digit vs digit+word differs after Holm correction (p = 0.046875); remaining comparisons do not (Holm p = 0.21875). Prompt-level format scores are not included. |
| The effect is specific to addition, or operators are equivalent. | Not established | [`equal_operand_operator_advantages.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_operator_advantages.csv); [`equal_operand_operator_pairwise.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_operator_pairwise.csv); [`equal_operand_plus_vs_others.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_plus_vs_others.csv) | Plus-versus-other adjusted p = 0.6875; no detection is not proof of equivalence. Non-addition strings still score the arithmetic sum token. |
| The parity-matched control independently establishes a positive mean preference. | Not established | [`equal_operand_parity_control_inference.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_parity_control_inference.csv) | Mean +0.1865; exact sign-flip p = 0.140625; 95% target-bootstrap interval [−0.0068, +0.3863]. The interval includes zero. |
| The nominated components establish a causal circuit. | Not established | [`equal_operand_causal_specificity.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_causal_specificity.csv); [`equal_operand_new_range_target_summary.csv`](../data/current/equal_operand_new_range_target_summary.csv) | FDR families cover 12 nominated components (one-sided q = 0.09375; two-sided q = 0.15); the five-target discovery screen has limited resolution. In exploratory transfer targets 24–36, signed advantage change decreases at 5/7 and increases at 2/7; unablated advantages at 26 and 34 are negative. |
| Corpus co-occurrence explains or rules out the equal-operand effect. | Unresolved | [`CORPUS_AUDIT.md`](CORPUS_AUDIT.md); raw logs and no-floor summary in `data/current/`; audit A32 | 79 requests, no logged failures, all seven joint log ratios positive; conditional ratios mixed. Post hoc seven-target correlations are joint ρ = +0.464 (p = 0.294) and conditional ρ = −0.143 (p = 0.760). The sample is small, the analysis was not prespecified, and Dolma is a proxy. |
